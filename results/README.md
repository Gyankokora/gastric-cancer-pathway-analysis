# Results

The principal reported findings were:

- 24 genes commonly upregulated across three GEO datasets.
- 60 genes commonly downregulated across the same datasets.
- Recurring enrichment in extracellular matrix, collagen, focal adhesion, and PI3K–Akt-related pathways.
- Significant Kaplan–Meier overall-survival associations for COL1A1, COL6A3, and THBS2.

Detailed analyses, figures and interpretation are presented in the project report.
